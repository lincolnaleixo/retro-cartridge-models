# Credits — NES — early US retail box template

Original parametric reconstruction and release: **Lincoln Aleixo**.

The geometry is authored from construction observations and the measurement basis recorded below. No reference photograph, scanned mesh, game artwork or copied manufacturer logo is embedded in this neutral template. Product design, brand names and any third-party rights remain outside the license for original contributions. Attribution does not establish a license in those separate elements.

## Reference basis

- [protector manufacturer fit dimensions](https://shop.retroshell.com/product/nintendo-nes-game-protector-case/). Approximate NES closed envelope; these are protector-fit dimensions, not Nintendo manufacturing specifications.
- [collector-authored specimen documentation with original photographs](https://www.thelegendofnes.com/cib). NES construction, including the selected early integrated-hangtab variant. Foam and sleeve dimensions are inferred to fit the existing cartridge envelope.
- [seller photographs of a physical early five-screw Super Mario Bros. specimen](https://www.alternateworldscomics.com/products/super-mario-bros-5-screw-nes-rare-first-edition-tabbed-box). Early integrated-hangtab edition distinction and overall proportions, cross-checked with the selected specimen photographs.
- [seller original photographs of an early no-REV-A physical Super Mario Bros. box](https://www.ebay.com/itm/385662571091). Selected NES early US exterior construction and print placement. Exact board gauge and die dimensions remain inferred.

The release includes `reference-evidence.json` with exact source URLs, observations, edition choices and measurement caveats. Dimensions, die cuts, foam size and paper gauge are inferred. The early hanger is a selected US variant, not a claim about every NES printing. Foam pore shading is procedural in the source; the portable export retains its clean base material.
