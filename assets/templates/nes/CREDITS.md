## Dimensional references

- [no$nes technical notes, Cartridge Shell Dimensions](https://problemkaputt.de/everynes.htm): approximate 119.7 × 134 × 16.75 mm shell, split between the front and rear halves, lower foot and connector opening. The source explicitly describes its shell measurements as approximate.
- [NESdev cartridge dimensions](https://www.nesdev.org/wiki/NES_cartridge_dimensions): measured PCB edge dimensions and an approximate 55 × 97 mm unfolded label with a 7 mm top fold. The cited board measurements describe an EWROM specimen; the model uses the common connector interface rather than that board's component layout.
- [Persune's label measurements](https://www.nesdev.org/wiki/User:Persune): 78.5 × 31 mm rear label measured on another licensed cartridge, corroborated visually against the five-screw specimen.
- [NESdev cartridge connector](https://www.nesdev.org/wiki/Cartridge_connector): 72 contacts and 2.5 mm pitch. The Famicom's 2.54 mm interface is not used here.

## Photographic reference

[Fenyx Games, Super Mario Bros. five-screw cartridge](https://www.fenyxgames.com/product-page/super-mario-bros-nes-5-screw-1985-nintendo) provides front and rear photographs of the early shell: left rib channel, top finger recess, folded label, five screw seats, stepped lower shoulders and rear warning-label land. Perspective photographs establish visible feature presence, not precise depths or radii. The seller's photographs, game artwork and Nintendo marks are not included in this neutral template, and no permission to redistribute them is asserted.

The shell and procedural ABS maps are original contributions. Neutral labels contain no copied pixels, and the rear mark land omits trademark lettering. The [evidence record](../../../docs/nes-reference-evidence.json) records source confidence, inferred dimensions and development validation. No factory-fit, exact color-match or electrical-function claim is made. See the root [NOTICE.md](../../../NOTICE.md).
