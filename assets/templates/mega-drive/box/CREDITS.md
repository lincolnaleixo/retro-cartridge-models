# Credits — Mega Drive — western clamshell box template

Original parametric reconstruction and release: **Lincoln Aleixo**.

The geometry is authored from construction observations and the measurement basis recorded below. No reference photograph, scanned mesh, game artwork or copied manufacturer logo is embedded in this neutral template. Product design, brand names and any third-party rights remain outside the license for original contributions. Attribution does not establish a license in those separate elements.

## Reference basis

- [Scanned original Sonic the Hedgehog PAL instruction manual, rehosted by Sonic Retro](https://info.sonicretro.org/images/1/15/Sonic1_MD_EU_manual.pdf). The aspect ratio is observed from the scan. Physical size, thickness and lateral placement remain estimates; no page artwork is included in the neutral template.
- [Original specimen photograph](https://www.tradera.com/item/340720/717550997/sonic-the-hedgehog-sega-mega-drive). Oblique photograph, no ruler; used for construction and relative layout only. Wear and manufacturing logos are not copied.
- [Firsthand auction catalogue description of an original specimen](https://darwenauctioneers.co.uk/auction/no-auction_number/lot-233-sonic-the/). Corroborates packaging construction and edition family, not the exact mold variant or dimensions.
- [Protector manufacturer's published fit specification](https://shop.retroshell.com/product/sega-megadrive-game-protector/). Search-indexed manufacturer text was accessible; direct product URL returned 404 during review. This is a protector fit envelope, not an original Sega manufacturing drawing.
- [Original Japanese case specimen photograph, construction comparison only](https://www.ebay.com/p/1974610380). Japanese specimen is not asserted to be the same PAL mold. Used only to understand structural features independently visible in the Sonic PAL photo.

The release includes `reference-evidence.json` with exact source URLs, observations, edition choices and measurement caveats. The 129 × 177 × 26 mm body and 188 mm height including hanger are nominal estimates from protector fit and specimen photographs. Mold revision, clip offsets, sleeve thickness and booklet pagination are approximate. Flexible spine folds use rigid pivots.
