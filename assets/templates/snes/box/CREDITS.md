# Credits — Super Nintendo — western retail box template

Original parametric reconstruction and release: **Lincoln Aleixo**.

The geometry is authored from construction observations and the measurement basis recorded below. No reference photograph, scanned mesh, game artwork or copied manufacturer logo is embedded in this neutral template. Product design, brand names and any third-party rights remain outside the license for original contributions. Attribution does not establish a license in those separate elements.

## Reference basis

- [protector manufacturer fit dimensions](https://shop.retroshell.com/product/nintendo-snes-game-protector/). Approximate landscape SNES closed envelope; width and height are oriented to the landscape artwork.
- [protector manufacturer product dimensions](https://retroprotection.com/product/snes-box-protector/). Independent approximate outer-fit cross-check only; protector dimensions include clearance and do not establish exact game-box sizes.
- [grading company photographs and inspection of a physical sealed Super Mario World specimen](https://www.cgcgrading.com/news/articles/the-story-behind-a-sealed-super-mario-world-certified-by-cgc-video-games-4NhgeKqaMuFtMK4vDTFZfu/). US edition and landscape external packaging reference; not a measured die line.
- [seller photographs of a physical Super Mario World display carton and insert](https://discountgamesonline.com/products/super-mario-world-fdo-for-display-only-box-snes-super-nintendo). Folded insert construction only, not a claim that this specimen establishes retail insert measurements.
- [replacement insert supplier photographs](https://respawnandreplay.com/products/cartridge-cardboard-tray-for-super-nintendo-snes-super-famicom-inner-inlay-insert). Qualitative triangulated folds and finger-access opening; original tooling and fit are not measured.

The release includes `reference-evidence.json` with exact source URLs, observations, edition choices and measurement caveats. Protector-fit dimensions establish an approximate envelope. Paper gauge, tray aperture, flap lengths and folds are inferred from photographs, not measured factory die lines.
