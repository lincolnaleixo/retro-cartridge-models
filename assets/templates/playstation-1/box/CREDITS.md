# Credits — PlayStation 1 — clear-tray jewel case template

Original parametric reconstruction and release: **Lincoln Aleixo**.

The geometry is authored from construction observations and the measurement basis recorded below. No reference photograph, scanned mesh, game artwork or copied manufacturer logo is embedded in this neutral template. Product design, brand names and any third-party rights remain outside the license for original contributions. Attribution does not establish a license in those separate elements.

## Reference basis

- [Jewel-case manufacturer inspection criteria and component drawings](https://cmcdisc.com/media-packaging/original-jewel-case/). 142.35 × 124.75 × 10.4 mm nominal envelope chosen at the midpoints of the assembly ranges.
- [Original physical US black-label Crash Bandicoot specimen photographs](https://www.ebay.com/itm/306104343253). Clear-tray construction and edition-family cross-check, not dimensional measurements.

The release includes `reference-evidence.json` with exact source URLs, observations, edition choices and measurement caveats. The envelope uses midpoint values from CMC assembly inspection ranges, not proof of the original Sony supplier. Fine molded features, booklet leaves and paper-fit dimensions are approximate. Clear plastic rendering depends on the importing engine.
