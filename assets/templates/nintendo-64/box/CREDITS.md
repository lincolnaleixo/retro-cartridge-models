# Credits — Nintendo 64 — western retail box template

Original parametric reconstruction and release: **Lincoln Aleixo**.

The geometry is authored from construction observations and the measurement basis recorded below. No reference photograph, scanned mesh, game artwork or copied manufacturer logo is embedded in this neutral template. Product design, brand names and any third-party rights remain outside the license for original contributions. Attribution does not establish a license in those separate elements.

## Reference basis

- [protector manufacturer fit dimensions](https://shop.retroshell.com/product/nintendo-n64-game-protector/). Approximate landscape Nintendo 64 closed envelope; width and height are oriented to the landscape artwork.
- [grading company inspected physical specimens and original reference photographs](https://www.cgcvideogames.com/en-US/resources/complete-in-box-guide/nintendo-64). Avoid conflating later reprints with the existing early Super Mario 64 cartridge. Not dimensional evidence.
- [replacement insert manufacturer photographs](https://xyab.com/products/cardboard-box-insert-n64). Qualitative folded-stock cradle construction only. A modern replacement is not evidence of an exact Nintendo factory die line.

The release includes `reference-evidence.json` with exact source URLs, observations, edition choices and measurement caveats. Protector-fit dimensions establish an approximate envelope. The tray construction is informed by original and replacement examples; its die line and board thickness are not asserted to match a specific Nintendo factory revision.
