# Mega Drive western shell — references and scope

- Creator / attribution party: Lincoln Aleixo — https://github.com/lincolnaleixo
- Original parametric reconstruction: `tools/media_mega_drive.py`, generated with Blender.
- Original procedural surface maps: deterministic, periodic ABS normal and roughness images generated by the same module. They do not contain sampled photographs, downloaded texture assets or source-model image data.
- Original contributions: the collection's CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license. Existing Sega product design and trademarks remain outside that grant.

## Dimensional references

- [HD Retrovision's cartridge-shell reconstruction](https://www.hdretrovision.com/free-stuff/): independent hardware supplier's model of a physical non-EA Genesis shell. Selected scalar dimensions were extracted to corroborate the envelope, screw locations, rear grip and label recess. Its mesh is not imported into, traced vertex-for-vertex, or distributed with this reconstruction.
- [ConsoleMods label templates](https://consolemods.org/wiki/Cart_Labels): Genesis label 74 × 67 mm unfolded, 7 mm top fold, 2 mm corner radius; marked as test-printed and fitted against OEM media by its contributors.
- [Aaltomies' firsthand measurements](https://aaltomies.wordpress.com/2016/01/08/games-on-your-wall/): 17 mm cartridge thickness and connector-groove observations.

## Photographic references

- [Grillo Games: Sonic PAL specimen](https://www.grillogames.de/products/sega-mega-drive-spiel-sonic-the-hedgehog-modul-anleitung-ovp-sehr-guter-z), front/rear/top construction.
- [Genesis rear close-up](https://www.ebay.com/itm/296933836167), rear fasteners, grip and molded panels.
- [Genesis underside](https://www.ebay.com/itm/205354222286), recessed connector opening.

Photographs, commercial game artwork, logos and third-party meshes are not included. The labels and rear molded information areas remain neutral. Geometry uses corroborated nominal dimensions and inferred molding details, not Sega production CAD; it is not certified as a printable replacement shell.
