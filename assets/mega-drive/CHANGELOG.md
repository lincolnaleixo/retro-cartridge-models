# Mega Drive cartridge changelog

## Unreleased

- 0.1.0: packaged the reconstructed neutral western shell with original ABS textures, editable source, GLB and reviewed export views. Added local owner-label mapping for Sonic the Hedgehog.

- Replaced the Blender-only Noise/Bump ABS shader, which disappeared during GLB export, with original periodic normal and roughness PNGs. All three 512 × 512 maps are packed in the editable source and embedded in the GLB. An 8 mm repeating surface UV map preserves consistent grain scale separately from the artwork. The front remains satin; the rear is subtly more matte. Grain strength is a restrained visual approximation, with no copied texture or added scratches.
- Reduced hidden contact-pad geometry from beveled solids to thin planar-sided solids at the same dimensions and positions. The reconstruction now has 12,152 triangles, down from 23,416, while retaining the 108.972 × 70 × 17 mm exported envelope. Enabled backface culling for closed shell, PCB, contact and fastener materials after verifying manifold meshes and positive signed volume.
- Kept both replaceable artwork materials untextured and their UV0 domains at 0–1. A Godot import check caught UV remapping when the rear artwork shared the textured ABS material; the rear land now has its own neutral material so runtime artwork remains upright and unclamped.
- Verified the refined export by Blender round-trip and Godot 4.7.2 import: both artwork meshes preserve 0–1 UV0, the shell normal/roughness maps remain active, and solid materials retain backface culling. Compared the actual imported GLBs under identical close-up framing and lighting; the micrograin changes the highlights without adding visible wear. Python compilation and the catalog verifier passed.
- Replaced the rejected generic draft with the western Sega shell: reduced thickness from 20.66 to 17 mm, removed invented vertical rods and lower frontal cutout, rebuilt smooth cheeks and the stepped rear molding, and recessed the PCB inside the underside opening. Actual exported bounds are approximately 108.972 × 70 × 17 mm.
- Replaced four corner screws with two recessed security-shaped heads and connected shafts/bosses; added a real 1.7 mm-deep horizontal rear grip. Rebuilt the front artwork as a continuous 74 × 67 mm sheet with a 7 mm top fold in a 0.4572 mm recessed land. The rear artwork contract is now a neutral molded 77.75 × 21.75 mm panel.
- Added dimensional sources and confidence limits. Inspected actual-GLB hero/rear/top/underside renders; source ray checks confirmed the grip floor, label recess, intact lower front and recessed PCB. These checks concern visual geometry, not physical replacement-part certification.
- Validation of the replacement: actual export has 23,416 triangles in nine meshes; integrity/UV inspection and closed-world round-trip bounds passed. All six orthographic views were generated from the GLB. Godot 4.7.2 import, media-profile smoke and preview controls passed. The catalog verifier and nine GLB-inspector tests also pass; catalog validation is not visual approval.
- Visual reference audit (2026-10-02): rejected the draft for fidelity. The photographed western shell has two rear fasteners, a horizontal rear grip and a top-wrapping label; the draft invents four screws, vertical ribs, a broad front label and an exposed frontal connector. Its apparent wells are overlays, not cavities. The earlier validation below covers export integrity only. Geometry remains unchanged pending reconstruction; see `../../docs/reference-audit-2026-10-02.md`.
- Added an original editable procedural cartridge study with rounded shoulders, beveled front/rear shells, assembly seam, side grips, rear screw recesses and a real lower opening exposing a PCB and contact strips.
- Added separate neutral `FrontLabel` and `RearLabel` meshes with face-correct UVs for runtime artwork replacement. No copyrighted game art or logo mesh is included.
- Added local Blender/GLB generation and actual-GLB studio previews without inventing a public release version or changing immutable releases. The approximate envelope is 110 × 70 × 20.66 mm, in meters with Y-up and a bottom-center origin.
- Merged repeated static details by material in the GLB, reducing the export from 107 meshes to eight while keeping the Blender source parts editable. Label names and UVs remain separate.
- Validation: regenerated in Blender 5.2, inspected hero/rear/top renders of the actual optimized GLB, confirmed the imported world envelope against the source within 0.001 mm, passed the GLB integrity/UV inspector (28,704 triangles, eight material surfaces, no external resources/cameras/lights), and passed the unchanged catalog verifier.
- Known limits: approximate generic cartridge silhouette, dimensions and detail placement; no official CAD or engineering fit certification. Release packaging and publication are still maintainer work.
