# Credits — Mega Drive — Sonic the Hedgehog box reproduction

Original parametric model, artwork preparation and release: **Lincoln Aleixo**.

## Artwork sources actually used

- [sonic-eu-wrap](https://info.sonicretro.org/File:Sonic1_box_eu.jpg). SEGA; Sonic Retro scan uploaded by ScarredSun on April 18, 2009. Complete European cream/pink-grid wrap with English-first multilingual rear, code 670-1470-50S, UPC 4974365610098. Archive fair-use statement is not a redistribution license.
- [sonic-eu-manual](https://info.sonicretro.org/File:Sonic1_MD_EU_manual.pdf). SEGA; Sonic Retro manual archive. European manual supplied by parent task. Front and back only; pages and pixel dimensions recorded by the manual preparation workflow.
- [sega-clean-wordmark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SEGA_logo_(no_R_mark).svg). SEGA wordmark; vector archived by Wikimedia Commons, uploaded by Tkgd2007 / Coffeeandcrumbs; source attributed to Keane Ideas portfolio. Clean vector raster supplies the wordmark shape; restored period colors and white keylines are documented transformations.

The bundled `artwork-sources.json` identifies this edition's selected references. `artwork-provenance.json` records source and output hashes, source pixel dimensions and restoration operations. `artwork-embedding-report.json` verifies the exact prepared images applied to each print surface.

Original glyph contour reconstruction, clean measured grid, bounded paper/color restoration and original illustration denoise. Chrome plate silhouette and reflection bands are explicitly reconstructed; clean archived SEGA vector supplies the wordmark. Contours and flat colors are restored approximations of the original print, not recovered factory vectors. Original screenshots and shaded character art retain source-resolution limits. Colors are visually estimated. Original Sonic title and type contours are traced into antialiased flat ink; the decorative grid is measured and redrawn. These are restored approximations, not recovered factory vectors. Chrome Mega Drive plate silhouette and silver/gold reflection bands are reconstructed from the documented original logo; the archived clean SEGA wordmark supplies the logo lettering. Original shaded Sonic illustration and four rear screenshots retain source-resolution limits. Pink/cream paper fields and black fields are cleaned independently of character shading. Colors are visually estimated, not calibrated. Prepared-face resolution limits fine lettering and image detail; local cleanup cannot establish exact original print colors or recover unseen design information. No generated replacement game illustration or invented lettering is asserted.

## Geometry references

The independent neutral mega-drive box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. The 129 × 177 × 26 mm body and 188 mm height including hanger are nominal estimates from protector fit and specimen photographs. Mold revision, clip offsets, sleeve thickness and booklet pagination are approximate. Flexible spine folds use rigid pivots.
