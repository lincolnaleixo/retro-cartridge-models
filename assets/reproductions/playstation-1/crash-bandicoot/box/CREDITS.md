The bundled `artwork-sources.json` identifies this edition's selected references. `artwork-provenance.json` records source and output hashes, source pixel dimensions and restoration operations. `artwork-embedding-report.json` verifies the exact prepared images applied to each print surface.

Deterministic local scan descreen and documented face-specific pristine reconstruction. No scan detail is invented. Colors are estimated from uncalibrated references. NES original illustration and small photographed console wordmark remain optically softer than reconstructed edges. Complete four-part original SCUS-94900 print set, including both exterior rear spines. Separate luminance/chroma descreen reduces coarse scan grain; fine rear type uses a gentler kernel. Known plain-black spine and trim fields are restored while original artwork remains; scans are not color-calibrated print proofs. Clear tray is verified by the documented physical US specimen. Updated neutral-template clear-plastic alpha fallback preserves readability in Godot Compatibility while making molded edges visible; exact reflections vary by renderer. Prepared-face resolution limits fine lettering and image detail; local cleanup cannot establish exact original print colors or recover unseen design information. No generated replacement game illustration or invented lettering is asserted.

## Geometry references

The independent neutral playstation-1 box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. The envelope uses midpoint values from CMC assembly inspection ranges, not proof of the original Sony supplier. Fine molded features, booklet leaves and paper-fit dimensions are approximate. Clear plastic rendering depends on the importing engine.
