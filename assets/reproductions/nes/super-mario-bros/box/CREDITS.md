The bundled `artwork-sources.json` identifies this edition's selected references. `artwork-provenance.json` records source and output hashes, source pixel dimensions and restoration operations. `artwork-embedding-report.json` verifies the exact prepared images applied to each print surface.

Original scan pixels; perspective rectification, bounded tonal normalization, border repair and explicitly masked local inpainting. No generated art or typography. Upsampling does not restore absent print detail. Photograph lighting is estimated; colors are not a calibrated match to the physical ink. See per-face effective resolution. All six face layouts verified. Original front is approximately 590×840 effective pixels; thin edges are optically soft. Source photographs have warm lighting and wear. Black-field repair is local; no new glyphs, stars or character art are drawn. Clean Nintendo/MarioWiki REV-A front was rejected because it does not match the requested early box. Prepared-face resolution limits fine lettering and image detail; local cleanup cannot establish exact original print colors or recover unseen design information. No generated replacement game illustration or invented lettering is asserted.

## Geometry references

The independent neutral nes box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. Dimensions, die cuts, foam size and paper gauge are inferred. The early hanger is a selected US variant, not a claim about every NES printing. Foam pore shading is procedural in the source; the portable export retains its clean base material.
