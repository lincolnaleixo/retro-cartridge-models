## Geometry references

The independent neutral nes box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. Dimensions, die cuts, foam size and paper gauge are inferred. The early hanger is a selected US variant, not a claim about every NES printing. Foam pore shading is procedural in the source; the portable export retains its clean base material.
