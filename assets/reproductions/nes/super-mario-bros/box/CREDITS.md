Deterministic local scan descreen and documented face-specific pristine reconstruction. No scan detail is invented. Colors are estimated from uncalibrated references. NES original illustration and small photographed console wordmark remain optically softer than reconstructed edges. The original early non-REV-A front illustration and small photographed console wordmark retain limited optical detail. Enlargement does not recover missing print detail. Top and side title/Nintendo glyphs are recomposited from the matching original rear scan in the specimen layout; readable address and warranty wording is transcribed with an approximate narrow sans-serif. Fresh black fields replace photographed scuffs. Identifiable printed stars remain; explicitly bounded irregular abrasion and tiny empty-field scan debris are locally repaired. Color is estimated from uncalibrated references. Prepared-face resolution limits fine lettering and image detail; local cleanup cannot establish exact original print colors or recover unseen design information. No generated replacement game illustration or invented lettering is asserted.

## Geometry references

The independent neutral nes box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. Dimensions, die cuts, foam size and paper gauge are inferred. The early hanger is a selected US variant, not a claim about every NES printing. Foam pore shading is procedural in the source; the portable export retains its clean base material.
