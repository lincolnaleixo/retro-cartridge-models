## Artwork and photography sources

- Front: **Golden Apple Comics**, [Super Mario Bros. cartridge listing](https://goldenapplecomics.com/products/nintendo-nes-super-mario-bros-video-game-cartridge), [original front photograph](https://goldenapplecomics.com/cdn/shop/files/DSC_0066_ca2ce5b8-bcda-4a95-a694-a42229171ffb.jpg?v=1712862274). The 3,750 × 4,216 image shows the early front label with a round gold seal and “Made in Japan” at lower left. The associated rear specimen has five screws. The unrelated aftermarket ownership sticker in the listing's rear photograph is not used.
- Folded top title: **Fenyx Games**, [five-screw Super Mario Bros. listing](https://www.fenyxgames.com/product-page/super-mario-bros-nes-5-screw-1985-nintendo), [front photograph showing the fold](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/872c00_4c2d64d6a1ff4aad8481eb1800fa77a1~mv2.png). The visible top region is small and oblique; fine lettering has lower fidelity than the main front label.
- Rear: **Fenyx Games**, the same listing, [rear photograph](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/872c00_0f82c9c322df4da69ac0629fa7285d1f~mv2.png). It shows the early five-screw warning label without REV-A. A later three-screw rear label is not substituted.

These seller photographs are identified as the source material; permission to redistribute them has not been established. Nintendo artwork rights are separate from photography rights. The photographs retain their original pixels and are selected through UV mapping, with no generated, recolored or retouched artwork. Source hashes and applied UV coordinates belong in the generated artwork provenance. Packing a photograph inside a model does not change its license scope.

The maintainer requested this specific reproduction as part of the publication workflow on **2026-10-15**. That request and these credits do not transfer or establish publication rights in Nintendo artwork, marks or the Golden Apple Comics and Fenyx Games photographs. No redistribution license for those elements is asserted; they remain outside the collection's CC BY-NC-ND grant. The repository's publication-rights requirement applies separately from technical package validation.

The front, fold and rear come from different photographs and may differ in color, wear and perspective. Shell measurements and molding are approximate, and no exact printed-color match is claimed. Read the root [NOTICE.md](../../../../NOTICE.md) and [LICENSE](../../../../LICENSE).
