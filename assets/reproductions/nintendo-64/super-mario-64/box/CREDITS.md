The bundled `artwork-sources.json` identifies this edition's selected references. `artwork-provenance.json` records source and output hashes, source pixel dimensions and restoration operations. `artwork-embedding-report.json` verifies the exact prepared images applied to each print surface.

Deterministic print reconstruction: selectively remove halftone grain, rebuild flat fields and exact transcribed text, preserve original illustration contours and gameplay scenes. No generative imagery. Digital reconstruction, not an untouched original scan. Colors and replacement typefaces are visual approximations, not calibrated factory inks. Original gameplay screenshots retain their source-resolution limit. The quality seal and recycling symbol are documented geometric reconstructions of the original marks. Duotone edge illustrations are source-based reconstructions with approximate placement and ink colors, not newly discovered flat original scans. Nintendo Classics front source remains 1548 × 1080 pixels; original rear comes from the 4170 × 2916 scan. Small jump illustration is 330 × 330 pixels; enlargement cannot add source detail. Replacement typography, seal/recycle geometry and warm background repairs are visual approximations, not calibrated factory print. Prepared-face resolution limits fine lettering and image detail; local cleanup cannot establish exact original print colors or recover unseen design information. No generated replacement game illustration or invented lettering is asserted.

## Geometry references

The independent neutral nintendo-64 box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. Protector-fit dimensions establish an approximate envelope. The tray construction is informed by original and replacement examples; its die line and board thickness are not asserted to match a specific Nintendo factory revision.

## Enclosed game media

The matching [nintendo-64-super-mario-64 v0.3.0](https://github.com/lincolnaleixo/retro-cartridge-models/releases/download/nintendo-64-super-mario-64-v0.3.0/nintendo-64-super-mario-64-0.3.0.zip) is enclosed at its original scale beneath a removable `Contents` parent. The standalone cartridge/disc release remains unchanged. Contact positions are approximate rigid visualizations; flexible paper and plastic deformation are not simulated.
