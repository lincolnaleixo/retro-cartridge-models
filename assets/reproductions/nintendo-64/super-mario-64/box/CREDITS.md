# Credits — Nintendo 64 — Super Mario 64 box reproduction

Original parametric model, artwork preparation and release: **Lincoln Aleixo**.

## Artwork sources actually used

- [sm64-1904-full-2](https://1904comics.com/products/n64-super-mario-64-us). Nintendo; original physical specimen photography by 1904 Comics. Original K-A US box. Top is purple Mario, bottom green Mario, left gold penguins, right red Bowser. Lighting is photographed and uncalibrated.
- [sm64-1904-full-4](https://1904comics.com/products/n64-super-mario-64-us). Nintendo; original physical specimen photography by 1904 Comics. Original K-A US box. Top is purple Mario, bottom green Mario, left gold penguins, right red Bowser. Lighting is photographed and uncalibrated.
- [sm64-front-cover](https://cibby.app/games/super-mario-64-n64/). Nintendo; Cibby archive; scan contributor not identified. Original US K-A front. Cibby rear on the same page is a later 1998 edition and was rejected.
- [sm64-launchbox-1927](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/images/204-super-mario-64). Nintendo; LaunchBox Games Database; individual scan contributor not identified. Printed-art layout cross-checked against physical specimen; database category alone was not treated as authenticity evidence.
- [sm64-launchbox-1952581](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/images/204-super-mario-64). Nintendo; LaunchBox Games Database; individual scan contributor not identified. Printed-art layout cross-checked against physical specimen; database category alone was not treated as authenticity evidence.

The bundled `artwork-sources.json` identifies this edition's selected references. `artwork-provenance.json` records source and output hashes, source pixel dimensions and restoration operations. `artwork-embedding-report.json` verifies the exact prepared images applied to each print surface.

Original scan pixels; perspective rectification, bounded tonal normalization, border repair and explicitly masked local inpainting. No generated art or typography. Upsampling does not restore absent print detail. Photograph lighting is estimated; colors are not a calibrated match to the physical ink. See per-face effective resolution. All six face layouts verified. Top, bottom and right derive from underexposed specimen photographs. No high-resolution flat scan of those faces found. Front 1044×732 is softer than the 4170×2916 rear. Rectification and enlargement do not add print detail. Prepared-face resolution limits fine lettering and image detail; local cleanup cannot establish exact original print colors or recover unseen design information. No generated replacement game illustration or invented lettering is asserted.

## Geometry references

The independent neutral nintendo-64 box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. Protector-fit dimensions establish an approximate envelope. The tray construction is informed by original and replacement examples; its die line and board thickness are not asserted to match a specific Nintendo factory revision.
