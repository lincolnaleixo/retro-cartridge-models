Deterministic print reconstruction: selectively remove halftone grain, rebuild flat fields and exact transcribed text, preserve original illustration contours and gameplay scenes. No generative imagery. Digital reconstruction, not an untouched original scan. Colors and replacement typefaces are visual approximations, not calibrated factory inks. Original gameplay screenshots retain their source-resolution limit. The quality seal and recycling symbol are documented geometric reconstructions of the original marks. Digital reconstruction, not an untouched original scan or a factory-calibrated print. Colors, replacement typography, quality-seal geometry and gray stripe motif are visual approximations. Original rear screenshots remain limited by the 1143 × 800 source; the original Dry Bones artwork is 429 × 438 pixels. 4K preparation improves raster output and fresh typed/vector details; it does not create new source illustration information. Prepared-face resolution limits fine lettering and image detail; local cleanup cannot establish exact original print colors or recover unseen design information. No generated replacement game illustration or invented lettering is asserted.

## Geometry references

The independent neutral snes box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. Protector-fit dimensions establish an approximate envelope. Paper gauge, tray aperture, flap lengths and folds are inferred from photographs, not measured factory die lines.
