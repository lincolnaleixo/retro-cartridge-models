The bundled `artwork-sources.json` identifies this edition's selected references. `artwork-provenance.json` records source and output hashes, source pixel dimensions and restoration operations. `artwork-embedding-report.json` verifies the exact prepared images applied to each print surface.

Original scan pixels; perspective rectification, bounded tonal normalization, border repair and explicitly masked local inpainting. No generated art or typography. Upsampling does not restore absent print detail. Photograph lighting is estimated; colors are not a calibrated match to the physical ink. See per-face effective resolution. All six face layouts verified. Front is a 2460×1716 scan; remaining faces derive from original specimen photographs. Complementary edge photographs retain the original typography and character poses. The top has shell-riding Mario; bottom has caped Mario. Rear has creases near its upper screenshots; fine print and screenshot detail are limited by the photograph. Prepared-face resolution limits fine lettering and image detail; local cleanup cannot establish exact original print colors or recover unseen design information. No generated replacement game illustration or invented lettering is asserted.

## Geometry references

The independent neutral snes box release supplies the construction. Its relevant sources and measurement caveats are retained in `geometry-reference-evidence.json`. Protector-fit dimensions establish an approximate envelope. Paper gauge, tray aperture, flap lengths and folds are inferred from photographs, not measured factory die lines.
