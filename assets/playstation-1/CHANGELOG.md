# PlayStation 1 jewel case and CD changelog

## Unreleased

- 0.1.0: changed the primary representation to a standalone CD. Removed the case from default generation, preserved the 15 mm hole and dark reading face, and added a separate annular FrontLabel. No rear artwork surface. Five meshes, 8,704 triangles; owner-provided Crash Bandicoot artwork supported separately.

- Refined the clear polystyrene with lower alpha and roughness, outward-facing culling and corrected solid orientation. This reduces layered haze in Godot Compatibility while retaining transmission for other renderers; transparency still depends on scene lighting and object sorting.
- Added original embedded roughness maps for the dark CD's subtle optical zones and neutral booklet paper. Kept optical variation out of the base color and the central annulus physically separate from the reflective layer. Replaced the solid booklet edge block with eight slightly inset page sections inside the same 1 mm envelope; retained all four internal clips, label UVs, hinge and disc clearances.
- Refinement validation: closed/open GLBs pass the integrity and UV inspector with 32,452 triangles, 15 meshes, 16 surfaces and two embedded images. Godot 4.7.2 imports the roughness maps through channel G and enables backface culling on the four transparent materials. An independent connectivity/volume check confirms all transparent components and eight booklet sections are closed and outward-oriented. Actual exported open-case and disc-underside renders were inspected; original nominal dimensions and interaction names are unchanged.
- Replaced the rejected generic case with the NTSC-U/C standard clear-tray variant, using CMC's 142.35 × 124.75 × 10.4 mm midpoint envelope and identified Silent Hill specimen photos. Rebuilt thin polystyrene walls, transparent corrugated strip, molded tray webs and four rounded booklet tabs; removed the black solid spine and invented corner blocks.
- Moved the rear insert inside the plastic and gave it a continuous 151 × 118 mm UV layout across both folded spines. Added a separate booklet edge block; the front artwork is now 120 × 120 mm. Rebuilt coaxial pin/socket hinges with relieved mating walls and slotted retaining fingers connected to the tray.
- Rebuilt the 120 mm disc as disjoint reflective and transparent radial regions, retaining the dark data underside and true 15 mm hole. Added a standalone CD GLB and front/underside render views; identified disc components separately from the fixed tray hub.
- Documented primary manufacturer/ECMA dimensional sources and the limits of photograph-derived fine features. The old rejection below refers to superseded geometry, not the current parametric reconstruction.
- Validation of the replacement: exported/reimported closed world bounds match 142.35 × 124.75 × 10.4 mm; closed/open GLBs pass the integrity/UV inspector (31,136 triangles, 15 meshes, 16 material surfaces). Inspected closed/open/rear/orthographic and disc-underside renders. An independent evaluated-mesh check found no intersections in 16 critical wall/rail, hinge-ear/pin and hub-finger/disc pairs. Godot 4.7.2 import, media-profile smoke and preview controls passed. Fine rib count/pitch and plastic appearance remain photograph-based approximations.
- Visual reference audit (2026-10-02): rejected the draft for fidelity and assembly. PAL and NTSC specimen details were mixed without a regional target; the rear paper is outside the plastic, the disc's clear center overlays metal, the lid pivot is 1.2 mm off the pin axis, and the hub is disconnected from the tray. The earlier validation below covers export integrity only. Geometry remains unchanged pending reconstruction; see `../../docs/reference-audit-2026-10-02.md`.
- Added an original editable single-CD jewel case with a clear hinged lid, beveled molded frame, black ribbed spine, tray, retaining fingers and hinge pins.
- Added a separate 120 mm annular CD with a 15 mm center hole, silver printable face, dark data underside, metallic matrix rings and transparent center hub. A separate open showcase export exposes the seated CD; the main export remains closed for shelf display.
- Added neutral `FrontLabel` and `RearLabel` meshes with face-correct artwork UVs. The front insert follows the named `FrontLid` hinge, allowing runtime opening without rebuilding the model.
- Added local Blender/GLB generation and actual-GLB studio previews without inventing a public release version or changing immutable releases. The approximate closed envelope is 142.025 × 125 × 10.325 mm, in meters with Y-up and a bottom-center origin.
- Merged static export geometry by material and parent while preserving editable source pieces and the live lid pivot. Added low-alpha transparency alongside transmission to keep the front insert visible in engines without transmission support.
- Validation: regenerated in Blender 5.2 and inspected actual-GLB hero/rear/top/open renders. Confirmed the closed imported world envelope against the source within 0.001 mm. Both GLBs passed the integrity/UV inspector (22,606 triangles, 14 meshes, 15 material surfaces, no external resources/cameras/lights), and the unchanged catalog verifier passed.
- Known limits: generic single-disc case dimensions and molded details, not a measured regional PlayStation packaging replica. Transmission appearance depends on engine support and lighting. Release packaging and publication are still maintainer work.
