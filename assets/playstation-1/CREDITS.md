## Dimensional references

- [CMC original jewel-case specifications](https://cmcdisc.com/media-packaging/original-jewel-case/): assembly width 142.20–142.50 mm, height 124.60–124.90 mm and depth 10.40 ± 0.2 mm, with component drawings and tray inspection dimensions. The midpoint envelope used here is a standard-case reference, not a claimed Sony tooling specification.
- [ECMA-130, second edition](https://ecma-international.org/wp-content/uploads/ECMA-130_2nd_edition_june_1996.pdf): 120 mm disc, nominal 15 mm center hole and 1.2 mm thickness.
- [Avery standard jewel-case insert](https://www.avery.ae/product/cd-cover-and-inserts-c32250-25): 151 × 118 mm unfolded rear paper size.
- [B.R. Productions CD packaging](https://www.bluray-factory.com/en/verpacken/for-cds.html): independent standard-case size corroboration and polystyrene enclosure material.

## Photographic references

- [Grillo Games: Silent Hill NTSC-U/C specimen](https://www.grillogames.de/en/products/sony-playstation-1-spiel-silent-hill-ovp-anleitung-ntsc-u-c-usa-konami), front, spine, rear and open case. Its visible clear tray and standard case are the selected visual variant.
- [Tekken disc underside](https://www.ebay.com/itm/335443828959), dark reflective data-side appearance.

Reference photographs and commercial artwork are not included. All paper surfaces and the printable disc face remain neutral. The embedded 1024 × 1024 disc optical-zone and 256 × 256 paper roughness maps are original deterministic procedural textures generated by the module, not copies or crops of photographs. The disc pattern changes roughness only; its visible bands are an optical approximation rather than a scan of encoded tracks.

Molded details, hinge clearances and booklet thickness are inferred from the photographed specimen and standard-case drawings; no factory-fit certification is claimed. This asset does not represent PAL branded cases, longboxes or multidisc packaging.
